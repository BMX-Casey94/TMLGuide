# Fact-check — Tomorrowland Belgium research notes

Fact-check date: 2026-11-18. Checked by an independent reviewer for TMLGUIDE v3.
TMLGUIDE is an independent fan guide and is not affiliated with Tomorrowland / WeAreOne.World B.V.

## Method

- Every claim below was checked against a source that was actually opened during this review. Official Tomorrowland
  properties (`belgium.tomorrowland.com`, `tomorrowland.com`, the official FAQ, the official add-on pages) and primary
  operator pages were preferred. Internet Archive copies of official pages were used only where the live page has since
  been rewritten for the next edition, and are labelled as such in the Evidence column.
- Verdicts: `confirmed` (an official/primary source states the same thing), `changed` (same fact, different value for
  the current cycle), `outdated` (the note is explicitly scoped to an older edition and a newer official value exists),
  `unverifiable` (no official/primary source found, or only community sources; nothing invented), and
  `contradicted-by-sources` (an official source states the opposite).
- Community sources (fan wikis, Reddit, blogs, third-party guides) are never used to support `confirmed`. Where a note
  rests only on such a source the verdict is `unverifiable` and the source is named as community.
- The notes are largely 2025- and 2026-scoped. The official Belgium pages now describe **2027** (Weekend 1:
  16–18 July 2027; Weekend 2: 23–25 July 2027). Prices are therefore stated with the year they apply to; a 2025 figure
  is not treated as confirmed for 2027.
- Out of scope by instruction (owned by another checker): 2027 ticket-sale dates and headline festival statistics,
  re-entry rules, the TicketSwap "official" claim, locker prices, Montagoe breakfast, the party tent, Winter Pearl
  prices and the Brasil half-price arithmetic. Those claims are not assessed here.
- No login, purchase, paywall or bot-check bypass was attempted. `faq.tomorrowland.com` article bodies are behind a
  sign-in wall for this client; where that happened the article's public excerpt is cited, not its full text.
