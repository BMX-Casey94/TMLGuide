# Fact-check: Tomorrowland Winter (Alpe d'Huez)

Checked on 2026-11-18. Scope: every file in `research/winter/`. Target edition: Tomorrowland Winter 2027, 20–27 March 2027.

## Method note

- Each quoted claim was checked against an official Tomorrowland Winter page (`winter.tomorrowland.com`, `press.winter.tomorrowland.com`, the official FAQ at `faq.tomorrowlandwinter.com`) or a primary operator page (Alpe d'Huez tourist office, SATA/skipass, Transaltitude, Ben's Bus, Altibus, French road-safety authority). Every URL in the tables was opened during this check; nothing is cited from a search-result snippet alone.
- Our notes are built on `tml.guide`, a fan site last updated 2024–2025. A `tml.guide`-derived figure that matches only itself is **not** confirmed. Where no official or primary page states a figure, the verdict is `unverifiable`, even where the figure is plausible.
- Fan sites, Reddit and blogs are not used as support. Where a claim rests only on such material the verdict is `unverifiable`.
- Verdict key: `confirmed` = the source states the same thing; `changed` = the fact holds but the value/date/price differs for 2027; `outdated` = the claim describes a past edition and no longer applies; `unverifiable` = no official or primary source found; `contradicted-by-sources` = a source states something materially different.
- **All prices are labelled with the season they apply to.** Figures carried over from 2025 or 2026 are marked as past-season and are not 2027 prices. 2027 festival-ticket prices are on sale now; 2027 package minimum prices are not published (packages show as "Sold out"); the 2027 Add-On Sale opens 26 November 2026, so most 2027 add-on prices below come from the published 2027 add-on pages where they exist.
- Excluded by instruction (owned by another agent): 2027 ticket-sale dates, headline attendance and artist statistics, the re-entry rule, the TicketSwap "official" claim, locker prices, Montagoe breakfast, party tent, Winter Pearl prices.
- No login, purchase, paywall or bot-check was bypassed.

**Sources that blocked or resisted this check.**

- `faq.tomorrowlandwinter.com` root and section index returned HTTP 500; the FAQ was read through its public article pages and its search JSON endpoint instead.
- `shop.tomorrowland.com` returned HTTP 500 and was not usable.
- The 2027 ticket-type sub-pages `/en/tickets/festival-ticket/4-day/`, `/1-day/` and `/en/tickets/prices/` returned 404, so official 2027 prices for the 4-day and 1-day Festival Tickets could not be read from an official page. They are recorded as `unverifiable`; the 7-day price was readable on the French ticket page.
- Several Alpe d'Huez tourist-office content pages rendered navigation shells with no body text. Where that happened, the official resort map PDF (`PLAN-Station_alpedhuez-hiver_2026_2027.pdf`), the lift operator `skipass.alpedhuez.com`, or the operator PDF was used instead.
- `securite-routiere.gouv.fr`'s winter-equipment page returned 404; the rule was verified on `service-public.gouv.fr`.
- SNCF Connect's luggage help page returned 404, so the name-tag claim is left unverified.
- `altibus.com`'s English destination page returned 404; the French Alpe d'Huez shuttle page was used.
