# TMLGUIDE v3 — Fact-check: contested and high-stakes claims

- **Checked:** 18 November 2026 (all retrievals dated 2026-11-18)
- **Method:** official Tomorrowland sites and the official Tomorrowland help centre (`faq.tomorrowland.com`, a Zendesk instance whose public articles were read directly); Brazilian federal law on `planalto.gov.br`. Fan sites were treated as leads only and are never the basis for "confirmed".
- **Scope:** only the claims listed below. No purchases, no logins, no attempt to bypass bot protection. Where a page failed twice it is recorded as blocked.
- **Note on staleness in our own notes:** every `research/` file was gathered on 2026-07-02 and several describe the 2025 or 2026 edition. The 2027 cycle pages went live in late 2026, so most date and rule claims have moved on.

## 1. 2027 Belgium sale dates, audiences, time zone, festival weekends, queue rules

### What our notes say
- `research/belgium/dates.md` — has no 2027 data at all. Its newest entry is the 2025 sales timeline (Global Journey 18 Jan 2025 17:00, Worldwide Pre-Sale 25 Jan 2025 17:00, Worldwide Sale 1 Feb 2025 17:00) plus a "recurring pattern" saying GJ falls mid-to-late January and the Worldwide Sale one week after the pre-sale.
- `research/belgium/overview.md` — a nine-step 2025 sale order: NFT Pre-Sale → Global Journey / Discover Europe → First 20 → Belgian-only sale → Worldwide Pre-Sale → Friendship Garden → Worldwide Sale → Travel partners → Waiting List.
- `research/general/faq.md` — maximum 4 passes per person per order; "no public schedule for line-up/host announcements".
- `research/belgium/pricing-tickets.md` — pre-sale vs worldwide sale price columns for 2025 only.

### What the notes disagree on
- The 2025 pattern in `dates.md`/`overview.md` implies a Belgium-only sale and a separate "First 20" moment in the 2027 cycle. The official 2027 pages list only three public sale moments and describe the pre-sale and general sale as worldwide. There is no Belgian-only date and no "Friends" date among the three.
- The notes put Global Journey in "mid-to-late January"; for 2027 it is 30 January.

### Verdict
**Changed.** The owner's reported dates and 17:00 CET start times are correct, but the owner's list omits the pre-registration window and the audience labelling needs correcting: 30 Jan is Global Journey packages only, 6 Feb is the Worldwide Pre-Sale, 13 Feb is the Worldwide (general) Ticket Sale. The official pages list three sale moments only; there is no Belgian-only date and no separately dated Friends date. Friendship Garden *packages* still exist — they are named as the exception to the ticket cap — but no friendship-specific sale date is published on the 2027 pages.

### Resolved value
- **Pre-registration:** opens 2 December 2026, 15:00 CET; closes 12 February 2027, 15:00 CET. Mandatory to enter any sale. Pre-registration does **not** guarantee a ticket.
- **Global Journey Travel Packages Sale:** Saturday 30 January 2027, 17:00 CET. Packages include a Tomorrowland ticket plus hotel and/or DreamVille, with or without transport. Global Journey has **no** Exchange Desk; those packages are refundable under the cancellation conditions instead.
- **WorldWide Pre-Sale:** Saturday 6 February 2027, 17:00 CET. Tickets and DreamVille packages.
- **WorldWide Ticket Sale:** Saturday 13 February 2027, 17:00 CET. General sale of tickets and DreamVille packages.
- **Time zone:** CET as published on every official 2027 page. Note the trap: the Exchange Desk deadline is quoted as **CEST** (29 April 2027, 23:59 CEST) because by then Belgium is on summer time. Do not convert CET to CEST blindly.
- **Festival weekends 2027:** Weekend 1 = 16–18 July 2027; Weekend 2 = 23–25 July 2027 (official Belgium homepage). DreamVille therefore opens Thursday 15 July and Thursday 22 July 2027, and closes Monday 12:00 after each weekend (official opening-hours article). *The Thursday-open wording is inferred from the standing "DreamVille is open continuously from Thursday 11:00 until Monday 12:00" rule, not from a 2027-specific line.*
- **Queue / registration rules:** you must pre-register inside your Tomorrowland Account to gain access to the sales; on the sale day you sign in and enter the ticket shop. Everyone who registered may join all three sale moments. Tomorrowland "strongly recommends" creating the account at least 2 hours before the sale starts.
- **Ticket cap (resolved for 2027):** maximum **4 people per Tomorrowland Account**, cumulated across all sale moments, except Friendship Garden packages. During the NFT sale the cap is 2 people. Global Journey packages: maximum **6 people** per account, 2 during the NFT Global Journey sale. The `faq.md` figure of 4 per order is therefore right, but "per order" is the wrong unit — it is per account, across all sales.
- **Name changes:** not permitted on tickets. Global Journey allows exceptions in urgent cases until 29 April 2027, 23:59 CEST, at €350 per name.
- **Conflict inside the official site (record it, do not resolve it silently):** `sales-info/how-to-order` says the Exchange Desk closes "April 29, 2027 - 12:00 CEST"; the FAQ article `360037588571` says "April 29, 2027 - 23:59 CEST". Publish "29 April 2027" without a clock time, or publish 23:59 and note the discrepancy.
- **Fee:** a €10 fee per ticket is deducted from Exchange Desk refunds, processed within 8 weeks of sale. Listing on the Exchange Desk is final and cannot be undone; resale is not guaranteed.
- Headline statistics: see item 1b below.

## 2. Re-entry at the festival and at DreamVille

### What our notes say
- `research/belgium/overview.md` — "Non-DreamVille bracelets: main entrance only; leaving via main entrance = no re-entry the same day."
- `research/general/faq.md` — flags that re-entry is not covered by the fan FAQ and "needs dedicated verification from official FAQ before publishing".

### What the notes disagree on
- Nothing internally, but `overview.md` states the no-re-entry rule as if it applied only to non-DreamVille bracelets. The official rule is broader: it applies to everyone, including 3-day Full Madness holders.

### Verdict
**Confirmed and extended.** The no-re-entry claim is right, but it is a festival-wide rule, not an entrance-type rule, and DreamVille guests have two specific crossing windows.

### Resolved value
- **Festival:** "For safety reasons, after leaving Tomorrowland, you will not be able to re-enter on the same day, even if you have a Tomorrowland Bracelet for 3 days. OUT = OUT." This is the official headline rule.
- **Day Pass (Regular/Comfort):** no re-entry; a new entry ticket is needed.
- **Full Madness Pass (Regular/Comfort):** re-entry only on the following day; never the same day.
- **DreamVille ↔ festival:** you may re-enter DreamVille from Tomorrowland **from 17:00 onwards**; you may re-enter Tomorrowland from DreamVille **until 2 hours before closing time**. You are always free to leave and return to DreamVille itself.
- **Festival hours (same both weekends):** Friday and Saturday 12:00–01:00, Sunday 12:00–00:00. Check-in closes 22:00 on Friday and Saturday, 21:00 on Sunday; access denied after.
- **DreamVille:** open continuously Thursday 11:00 to Monday 12:00. Thursday check-in from 11:00 at both entrances; Friday 24 h; Saturday until 12:00 at both entrances, then DreamVille Entrance 1 (Nachtegaalstraat) only; **no check-in at all on Sunday**. Check-out by Monday 12:00, no exceptions.
- **Wristband identity rule:** if the name on the bracelet or ticket does not match your ID, access may be refused. Valid ID = identity card, passport or driving licence; copies and mandates are not accepted. Name changes are impossible after personalisation. This is the factual basis for the "name on the ticket proves nothing" line in our notes being only half right — the name does matter once personalised, because it cannot then be changed.

### Sources
- https://faq.tomorrowland.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037215812-Can-I-re-enter-the-festival — retrieved 2026-11-18. Quote: "even if you have a Tomorrowland Bracelet for 3 days. OUT = OUT".
- https://faq.tomorrowland.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037582731-What-are-the-opening-hours-of-the-festival-site — retrieved 2026-11-18. Quote: "Re-enter DreamVille from Tomorrowland: from 17:00 onwards".
- https://faq.tomorrowland.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037588511-Can-I-enter-if-the-registered-name-on-the-Bracelet-is-not-my-own — retrieved 2026-11-18. Quote: "Name changes are not possible after personalization."

### Confidence
**High.** All three statements are verbatim official FAQ text, updated 16–18 November 2026.

## 3. Is TicketSwap the official resale platform?

### What our notes say
- `research/general/faq.md` — "Exchange desk = the only official resale/refund channel"; lists Viagogo, StubHub, Ticketswap and eBay as "explicitly unsafe"; and carries an editorial flag saying "the brief mentioned 'official Ticket Swap' — per tml.guide this does not exist; only the exchange desk is official."

### What the notes disagree on
- Nothing internally — but the note's conclusion rests on a fan wiki, and the flag shows the claim has been contested. It needed the official source, which is what follows.

### Verdict
**Confirmed.** TicketSwap is not an official Tomorrowland channel. The official resale mechanism is the **Exchange Desk**, operated inside the main buyer's own Tomorrowland Account. Two separate official pages make this checkable: the Official Sales Channels article lists three authorised channels and TicketSwap is not among them, and an FAQ search for "TicketSwap" returns no results at all.

### Resolved value
- **Official sales channels (only these):** the Paylogic ticket shop (via my.tomorrowland.com); the Official Travel Shop for Global Journey packages (via my.tomorrowland.com); Official External Travel Partners.
- **Official resale mechanism name:** **Exchange Desk**. It sits in the main buyer's Tomorrowland Account. For 2027 it opens 13 February 2027 and closes 29 April 2027, 23:59 CEST.
- **Tickets cannot be cancelled.** All purchases are final.
- **Listing is not a sale.** Registering a ticket on the Exchange Desk does not guarantee anyone buys it.
- **Global Journey is excluded** from the Exchange Desk entirely; GJ packages are refundable only under the published cancellation conditions, and transport, accommodation or ticket-type changes are impossible after booking.
- **Name changes** are impossible after personalisation, so "just sell it to a friend and change the name" is not a route.
- **Wording safe for the site:** "The Exchange Desk, inside the main buyer's Tomorrowland Account, is the only official way to offer a ticket for resale. TicketSwap and similar marketplaces are not official channels."

### Sources
- https://faq.tomorrowland.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037586711-What-are-the-Official-Sales-Channels — retrieved 2026-11-18, updated 18 Nov 2026. Quote: "Please only order your Tickets & Packages via the Official Sales Channels".
- https://faq.tomorrowland.com/hc/en-us/search?query=TicketSwap — retrieved 2026-11-18. Quote: "No results for "TicketSwap"".
- https://faq.tomorrowland.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037588571-Can-I-cancel-or-resell-my-tickets — retrieved 2026-11-18. Quote: "Tickets cannot be cancelled. All purchases are final."

### Confidence
**High** that TicketSwap is not official and that the Exchange Desk is the official mechanism, both read off official pages. **Medium** on the precise Exchange Desk mechanics beyond the dates, because the linked detail article was not opened.

## 8. Brasil half-price (meia-entrada) rule and the maths in our notes

### What our notes say
- `research/brasil/dates-tickets.md` — Full Madness R$3,160 and "Full Madness Half-Price (meia-entrada) / PwD R$1,580"; "Full Madness Comfort (VIP) R$5,535" and "Full Madness Comfort Half-Price R$3,955"; Bybit Early Bird R$2,760 with "Bybit Early Bird Half-Price R$1,264"; and the line "Half-price (meia-entrada) eligibility per federal and São Paulo state law, proof required."

### What the notes disagree on
- Nothing internal, but the pricing table implies every "half-price" line is 50% of its full-price line. It is not, for the Comfort and Early Bird tiers (see below).

### Verdict
**Rule: confirmed. Arithmetic: partly wrong, and now over-stated in our notes.** The notes call the Comfort line a "half-price"; on the published figures it is about 71% of the Comfort price, not 50%.

### Resolved value — what is verified
- **The rule is real and official.** The Brasil ticket page states: "According to federal and state laws of São Paulo, those who meet the requirements and provide proof of eligibility are entitled to a half-price ticket."
- **It is restricted to Brazilian citizens.** The official Brasil FAQ article says the benefit "is available exclusively to Brazilian citizens, as it is granted under Brazilian law." Our notes do not say this. This is the most consequential omission: a UK or EU reader cannot buy a meia-entrada.
- **Eligible categories, per the official article:**
  1. Brazilian students, with a valid nationally standardised Brazilian CIE (digital certification) or equivalent student ID from an authorised body (ANPG, UNE, UBES, state/municipal entities, DCEs, academic centres). Bank slips, enrolment declarations and website screenshots are **not** accepted.
  2. People aged 60 or over, with valid government photo ID (Federal Law 10,741/2003).
  3. People with disabilities, plus one companion where required, with a BPC card or INSS document under Complementary Law 142/2013, plus ID; the companion also needs a declaration of need for assistance.
  4. São Paulo state public-school administrative professionals (State Law 15,298/14).
  5. São Paulo state public-school teachers (State Law 14,729/12).
  6. Low-income young adults aged 18–29 registered in Cadastro Único with family income up to two minimum wages, holding a valid ID Jovem.
- **Failure to prove it costs money:** if eligibility cannot be verified at entry, access is granted only after paying the difference to the full price in force on the festival date.
- **Federal law basis (primary source):** Lei nº 12.933/2013 guarantees students half of "o preço do ingresso efetivamente cobrado do público em geral" — half the price actually charged to the general public. It also guarantees people with disabilities and their companion (§8), low-income young people aged 15–29 (§9), and requires 40% of each event's tickets to be available at meia-entrada (§10). Art. 1 §1 states the benefit is **not cumulative** with other promotions.
- **Two points where our notes and the law diverge, without me giving a legal view:**
  - The law's low-income age band is **15–29**; the official Tomorrowland Brasil article says **18–29**. Both are recorded; the discrepancy is the operator's, not ours.
  - Art. 1 §1 says the benefit is not cumulative with other promotions. Our table carries a "Bybit Early Bird Half-Price", which is a discounted ticket with a half-price variant. Whether that is a cumulative benefit is a question for Tomorrowland Brasil, not something I can settle from the text.

### Resolved value — what is not verified
- **The per-tier arithmetic, now largely explained.** The Brasil ticket page renders prices as unlabelled runs of four figures per product, so I could not map every figure to a product name with certainty. But the runs reveal a consistent structure across three tiers:

  | Tier (inferred) | Full | "Half-price" | "Half-price" | "Social" |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Full Madness | R$3,160 | R$1,580 | R$1,580 | R$1,925 |
  | Full Madness Comfort | R$5,535 | R$3,955 | R$3,955 | R$4,300 |
  | Bybit Early Bird Full Madness | R$2,960 | R$1,380 | R$1,380 | R$1,725 |

- **The pattern: both discounts are flat rand amounts, not percentages.** Every "half-price" figure is the full price minus **R$1,580**. Every "social" figure is the full price minus **R$1,235**. The absolute difference is identical in all three rows, which is far too regular to be coincidence.
- **Consequence for the half-price rule:** the meia-entrada discount only equals a true 50% on the base Full Madness pass, because R$3,160 ÷ 2 = R$1,580. On Comfort it comes to 71.4% of the full price, and on the Bybit tier 46.6%. So the label "half-price" is accurate for the standard pass and misleading for the others. This is Tomorrowland Brasil's own published pricing, not an error in our notes.
- **Our notes' numbers, checked against the page:**
  - Full Madness R$3,160 and R$1,580 — **match exactly**.
  - Social R$1,895 — does **not** appear; the page shows R$1,925. Note that R$1,895 + the R$30 mandatory donation = R$1,925 exactly, so our figure may be the pre-donation price. That reconciles the two but is my inference, not a stated fact.
  - Comfort R$5,535 / R$3,955 / R$4,300 — **match exactly**.
  - Bybit Early Bird R$2,760 / R$1,264 / R$1,546 — **none matches** the page's R$2,960 / R$1,380 / R$1,725. Our R$1,264 is exactly 40% of R$3,160, which suggests the number was generated by a percentage calculation rather than copied from the page. Treat the whole Bybit row as unreliable and replace it.
- **An official inconsistency worth recording without adjudicating:** the Social Ticket article states a "40% discount on the full ticket price", but 40% off R$5,535 is about R$3,321, not the R$4,300 shown. The flat-discount reading explains the published prices; the percentage wording does not. Both are official; the site contradicts itself.
- **Day-pass half-price** is not published for 2027; our R$625 figure is a 2025 estimate.
- **Bybit eligibility.** The Early Bird required an active Bybit account and Bybit Card per our notes; the Brasil pages I opened did not restate that, so treat it as unverified for 2027.
