# Fact-check: Tomorrowland Brasil + general notes

**Checked:** 18 November 2026
**Scope:** every file in `research/brasil/`, plus `research/general/editions-dates.md`, `research/general/nft.md`, `research/general/radio.md`.
**Checker:** independent verification pass. TMLGUIDE is not affiliated with Tomorrowland / WeAreOne.World B.V.

## Method

- Each claim was read from the research file, then checked against an official or primary page that was actually opened during this pass. The URL in the `Official source URL` column is the page the evidence was read from, not a page inferred from a search result.
- Verdicts: **confirmed** (official source states the same thing), **changed** (official source now states a different value), **outdated** (was true for an earlier edition but is no longer the current position), **unverifiable** (no official or primary source could be reached or found; community sources only), **contradicted-by-sources** (official source states the opposite).
- Fan sites, blogs, Reddit and press aggregators were treated as community sources only. A claim resting solely on them is marked `unverifiable`, never `confirmed`.
- Prices and dates were checked against the live Brasil 2027 ticket pages on 2026-11-18. Where a page shows both a standard price and a `| Bybit` price, the standard price is used unless the claim is specifically about the early-bird sale.
- Request rate was kept at roughly one request per second. No logins, no purchases, no paywall or bot-check circumvention.
- One integrity note: the URL `press.brasil.tomorrowland.com/relive-the-magic-of-tomorrowland-brasil-2025` now serves a different release (the Agents of Time / Time Machine announcement) whose "About Tomorrowland Brasil" footer still carries the 2025 dates, theme and stage count. Where that page is cited, the evidence quoted is from that footer block.
- Items owned by another agent were skipped: 2027 Belgium ticket sale dates and headline statistics, re-entry rule, the TicketSwap "official" claim, locker prices, Montagoe breakfast, party tent, Winter Pearl prices. The Brasil half-price arithmetic is recorded below but marked as owned elsewhere.

### Notes on this file

- **Row 1–3 caveat.** The English statement "there will be no Tomorrowland Brasil in 2026 … not a cancellation, but a postponement" was issued by Tomorrowland spokesperson Debby Wilmsen and is reproduced verbatim in full by Gshow and EDMTunes. It was not found on a Tomorrowland-owned URL that is still live today: the September 2025 announcement page has since been replaced, and both `press.brasil.tomorrowland.com` and `brasil.tomorrowland.com` now describe a "year without a local edition". Rows 2 and 3 therefore cite the outlet that carries the official statement; row 1 cites the Tomorrowland-owned page that confirms the absence of a 2026 edition in its own words.
- **Row 2 citation note.** Gshow is a Globo news outlet, not a primary operator page. It is cited here only because it reproduces the full official statement with attribution to Tomorrowland's press office, which is the primary material.
- **Row 11 is the most consequential date error.** Global Journey went on sale **15 September 2026**. The 17 September date is still published on two official pages (`portaldoamanha.tomorrowlandbrasil.com` and the 3 May 2026 press release), but the definitive "everything you need to know" article and the 4 September 2026 press release both confirm 15 September. Treat 17 September as the originally announced date that was subsequently brought forward.
- Rows 22–27: all six Bybit early-bird prices in our notes are too low, and the pattern shows why. The notes appear to have applied the marketing line "up to 20% off, capped at R$400" to each standard price (R$3,160 − R$400 = R$2,760, etc.). The live page instead shows a **flat R$200 reduction** on Full Madness across standard, half-price and social tiers (R$3,160 → R$2,960; R$1,580 → R$1,380; R$1,925 → R$1,725). That flat R$200 is 6.3% of the standard Full Madness price, so the "up to 20%" headline describes the best-case saving on other eligible products, not the Full Madness discount. Do not restate "20% off" as the Full Madness early-bird discount.
- Row 29: confirmed via the Brasil help centre (article updated 11 August 2026): "40% de desconto … doação de BRL 30,00". The live Social price of R$1,925 against R$3,160 is a 39.1% reduction, consistent with 40% rounded.
- Row 15: the 6-per-buyer cap is confirmed. The Bybit sub-limit is **not** settled: two official Brasil FAQ articles contradict each other. Article `13511174562324` (edited 11 Aug 2026) says 2 per Account during the Bybit Pre-Sale; article `14233074960916` (edited 22 Sep 2026, i.e. the later edit) says there is no limitation for Inteira or Social beyond the 6-per-person cap. The later-edited article is the more likely current position, but the two were never reconciled, so do not publish the Bybit sub-limit as fact.
- Rows 30–31: 2027 day passes are already priced and mostly sold out. The note was written on the assumption that day passes come later.
