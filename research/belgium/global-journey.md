# What Global Journey is
- Official travel-package sale combining a Full Madness (or FM Comfort) pass with transport and/or accommodation; sold in its own January sale before the public sales.
- ~45,000 tickets were sold via GJ packages in 2025; generally the easiest way to secure tickets, but sells out within hours.
- Separate orders needed per weekend and per package type; parking is NOT included in GJ packages (even for Montagoe-tier lodgings).
- Common inclusions across packages: Full Madness (Comfort) pass, daily festival newspaper ("Tomorrowland Today"), souvenir bag.
- Flexible check-in/check-out (Wednesday to Tuesday) on flight/train/hotel packages, with tiered activities:
  - Arrive Wednesday → "Belgian Journey" day trip to a Belgian city on Thursday.
  - Arrive Thursday (Brussels hotel/hostel guests) → "Invited Brussels" pre-party on Thursday evening.
  - Depart Tuesday → "The Garden of Elements" recovery day on Monday (wellness activities).
- Hotel tiers across packages: hostels (dormitory, shared bathroom), Bronze/Silver/Gold hotels, Tomorrowland Theme Hotel (Brussels only), Concept Hotel.

# Bus packages
- Return coach to DreamVille + DreamVille accommodation only (no hotel option); includes Gathering access; GJ host at departure.
- 2025 departure cities (13 cities, 6 countries): Paris, Lille, Reims, Metz (FR); Eindhoven, Maastricht, Amsterdam (NL); Bonn, Düsseldorf, Aachen (DE); Luxembourg; London (UK); Copenhagen (DK).
- Exact departure point emailed ~2 weeks before travel.

# Train packages
- Return rail to Antwerp-Central or Brussels-Midi + transfers (station–accommodation–festival) + accommodation (DreamVille, hostel or hotel).
- 2025 departure cities: Paris, Düsseldorf, Amsterdam, London.
- "Party Trains" (on-board entertainment) from Amsterdam and Düsseldorf; flagged in the travel shop.

# Flight packages
- Return flight to Brussels Airport + transfers + accommodation (DreamVille, hostel, hotel, or hotel+DreamVille combo).
- Regular flights plus "Party Flights" with a Tomorrowland act on board (flagged in shop/price simulator).
- CRITICAL rule: not taking the outbound flight auto-cancels the return unless info@globaljourney.com is notified in advance.

# Hotel packages
- For people arriving by their own means; Brussels hotel + daily hotel–festival transfer.
- One shuttle departure per morning (12:00; theme hotels ~11:30 and 13:30); return via GJ shuttle hub (~45 min walk from Mainstage; last bus ~1 h after close).

# Friendship Travel
- Group package: B&B, house/villa, apartment or presidential suite + daily festival transfers.
- Unique perk: pick-up/drop-off anywhere within 200 km (~125 miles) of the chosen accommodation (e.g. from an airport).

# Discover Belgium (Weekend 1 only, 2025)
- 8 days / 7 nights, arriving Monday 14 Jul 2025; visits 4 cities: Antwerp, Bruges, Brussels, Ghent.
- Includes welcome gift, dedicated host, surprise activity + daily snack per city, all internal transfers, Belgian Journey on Thursday, daily festival transfers for hotel/hostel guests.
- Own transport to/from the first/last accommodation not included.

# Discover Europe
- Multi-city tour of 3, 4 or 5 European cities before/after the festival weekend, then Tomorrowland.
- Includes inter-city transport (flights; Amsterdam–Brussels leg by bus), checked + hand luggage, welcome/friendship drink and surprise activity per city, local host, travel guide with local/DJ recommendations, festival accommodation (DreamVille or Brussels hotel/hostel).
- Optional paid city activities in the add-on sale (2025 examples): Amsterdam walking tour €32, bike tour €39, pub crawl €25; Barcelona bike tour €38, city tour €60; Copenhagen beer tasting €56, boat tour €55; Ibiza Cala Comte €99, paddle-boarding €96; Lisbon sailing €60, surf lesson €65; Prague rafting €55, e-bike €46; Garden of Elements €75 (included when travelling onward to Ibiza/Prague/Barcelona).
- Hostels: 4–6 person dorm with en-suite; hotels: 3-star central or 4-star within ~15 min of the centre.
- Home-country transport to the first city / from the last city not included.

# Surprise Package (Weekend 2 only, 2025)
- 7 nights, Tue 22 Jul (starts Mechelen) → Tue 29 Jul (ends Brussels).
- Itinerary: 2 nights Martin's Klooster Leuven + 4 nights DreamVille Cabana + 1 night Cardo Hotel Brussels; FM Comfort pass, all transfers, surprise activities. Flights not included.

# Tomorrowland × Ridley cycling (Weekend 2, 2025)
- 21–28 Jul: 3-day cycling experience (21–24 Jul) then the festival.
- Rides: track session at Zolder velodrome; Flanders cobbles tour ~50 miles (~80 km, ~1,000 m climbing); gravel tour near Leuven ~37 miles (~60 km, ~600 m climbing).
- Includes: 3 nights cycling accommodation (all meals), 4 nights wellness concept hotel Brussels (breakfast), carbon gravel-bike loan + 30% purchase discount, bike transport, factory and wind-tunnel visits, personalised kit bag (jersey, bibs, cap, bottles, socks) with washing service, mechanical/medical support, daily festival transfers, Invited Brussels.
- Requires basic cycling experience.

# Youth Travel (under-26s; Weekend 2, 2025)
- 22–28 Jul: 2 nights Penta Hotel Brussels (22–24 Jul) incl. welcome evening; Ghent Festivities day trip with canal boat trip (23 Jul); Belgian Journey Thursday; then DreamVille Spectacular Friendship Garden with DIY tent package (24–28 Jul).
- Full Madness pass (no comfort), all transfers, group activities.

# DJ Academy hotel packages [VERY STALE — 2024 programme, 2023 prices]
- Week-long bootcamp (Mon–Thu) + festival weekend; Week 1 = beginner, Week 2 = advanced; stays at Le Plaza Brussels.
- Includes daily transfers, backstage tour, showcase on a Tomorrowland stage, surprise artist visits; ~2 h/day DJ workshop plus production and inspiration sessions.
- 2023 per-person prices: solo €2,980 (FM) / €3,333 (FM Comfort); duo €2,180 (FM) / €2,533 (FM Comfort).
- Academy-only tickets (no hotel/pass) sold via the Tomorrowland Academy site.
