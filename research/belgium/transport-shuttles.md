# Getting there — options
- Airports: Brussels Zaventem (closest major), Brussels South Charleroi (~2× the distance), Eindhoven (NL); Amsterdam Schiphol works via train (change Antwerp Central → Boom); Antwerp Airport is tiny (De Lijn bus to Antwerp Berchem, then train to Boom).
- Train: at least hourly service to Boom during the day; ~25 min signposted walk from Boom station to the main entrance.
- Car: separate parking for DreamVille and day/festival visitors; follow road signs not sat-nav (road closures); motorhomes banned from DreamVille car parks; single-entry parking tickets (leaving = buy a new one); on-the-day tickets cost up to €5 more than add-on sale ones.

# Brussels City Shuttle (2025)
- Round-trip only, €38, ~1 h each way.
- Outbound Fri/Sat/Sun from Brussels North station, half-hourly 10:00–14:00.
- Return from Velodroomstraat (right at main entrance) between ~5 min and 1.5 h after close; drop-offs: Brussels North, Central, South/Palace of Justice.

# Belgian/Dutch city shuttles (Keolis partnership, 2025; round trip only, valid one festival day)
- ~38 cities, €39–51 return. Examples: Kontich/Bornem €39; Mechelen/Berchem/Brussels Expo €40; Leuven €43; Ghent €45; Breda €45; Hasselt €46; Charleroi €47; Kortrijk €48; Bruges/Eindhoven/Maastricht/Rotterdam €49; Liège/Roeselare €50; Mons €51.
- City shuttles drop near the MAIN entrance (not DreamVille); return walk 15–20 min following City Shuttle signs — do NOT follow Global Journey shuttle signs (different location, vouchers not interchangeable).

# Boom station shuttles (free)
- Boom → DreamVille: Thu 09:00–23:00 (arrives Dirkputstraat) and Fri 09:00–20:00 (arrives Entrance 2); ~10 min ride.
- DreamVille → Boom: Monday 07:00–14:00 from Dirkputstraat. No station shuttle on Sunday.

# Night trains (after the festival)
- Extra trains to Antwerp only (stops: Antwerp Berchem, Antwerp Central; no onward Brussels connection).
- 2025 departures — Fri/Sat: 01:30, 02:00, 02:30, 03:00; Sun: 00:30, 01:00, 01:30, 02:00.
- Walk festival → Boom station ~20–30 min (turn right at exit, follow ground signs); trains fill front to back.
- Weekend Ticket (Sat/Sun): return trip at 50% discount; valid for the Sunday-night return even after midnight (staff sometimes wrongly deny this — SNCB refunds on complaint).
- Discounted festival train tickets (2025 from 2 July): codes to the main buyer — 1 per DreamVille/Day ticket, 3 per accommodation-free Full Madness; round trips only; GJ buyers excluded.

# Global Journey hotel shuttles
- One morning departure only, usually 12:00 sharp (theme hotels: two departures, ~11:30 and 13:30); missing it means own transport (train via Antwerp/Puurs ~€12 pp, or expensive taxi).
- Drop-off near the main entrance; return leaves from a shuttle hub ~2.5 miles away — ~45 min walk from the Mainstage; buses depart when full; last bus ~1 h after the final set.
- Shuttle-hub facilities: food trucks, toilets, free water en route, departure indicator board; hotel-specific shuttle bracelets checked (issued at hotel check-in).
- Free feeder shuttle from the roundabout south of the main entrance to the hub; stops ~30 min before the festival ends (may run longer in heavy rain).

# Taxis / ride-hailing
- Taxis cannot approach the site; dedicated taxi zone signposted from the main entrance (right turn).
- Pre-booked taxis: Kiss & Ride East only (not West); one K&R zone requires a shuttle.
- DreamVille pick-up/drop-off at Entrance 2 (Rumstestraat) possible off-peak, not guaranteed.
- Brussels Airport → festival roughly €100–150; night rates significantly higher; drivers charge festival premiums.

# Local buses
- De Lijn routes (e.g. 500 to Mechelen) are diverted during the festival; the 500 leaves from near the GJ shuttle hub (~45 min walk from Mainstage).
