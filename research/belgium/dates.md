# Recurring pattern (useful for planning content)
- Theme/date reveal: mid-to-late November.
- NFT sales: December.
- Global Journey sale: mid-to-late January (Saturday, ~17:00 CET).
- Worldwide Pre-Sale: late January (Saturday, 17:00 CET); Worldwide Sale one week later.
- Add-on sale: mid-June; timetable ~mid-to-late June.
- Waiting-list waves: roughly April–June.
- Aftermovie: released roughly August–December after the edition.
