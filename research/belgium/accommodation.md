# Common inclusions (every DreamVille package)
- Full Madness (or FM Comfort) pass per person, DreamVille access, access to The Gathering (Thursday opening party), Marketplace and Chill Out.
- Zone rule: guests can only enter their own accommodation zone; all types can use the Magnificent Greens common area.
- Proximity rule of thumb: pricier accommodation = closer to the festival gate (Magnificent Greens furthest, Mansion closest).

# Magnificent Greens (basic camping)
- Pick your own pitch; bring your own gear or buy a tent package.
- MG 1P Tent package: branded tent 250 × 150 × 120 cm, single air mattress, sleeping bag (collect at DreamVille entrance; take tent home afterwards).
- MG 2P Tent package: same tent, double air mattress, two sleeping bags.
- No power supply in the Magnificent Greens area.

# Camp2Camp (eco/social pre-pitched rental)
- Refurbished second-hand camping gear via social-employment scheme; deposit payable at collection, refunded if gear returned in good condition.
- 2P package: pre-pitched 2-person tent, double air mattress, 2 sleeping bags, 2 relax chairs.

# Friendship Garden (10-person group plots)
- Regular: private 10 × 10 m plot + one 365 × 365 cm shelter; 10 Full Madness passes.
- Spectacular: 13 × 13 m plot, shelter, 10 relax chairs, one shared locker in the FG locker area.

# Easy Tent (pre-erected, keep the tent afterwards)
- 2P: tent 265 × 265 × 205 cm, double air mattress, 2 sleeping bags, night light, mirror, tent lock.
- Spectacular 2P: adds wooden flooring, 1 power outlet (220 V EU), 2 relax chairs, locker 24 × 28 × 50 cm.
- Supreme 2P: custom tent 260 × 270 cm, two made-up beds, power outlets, patio with 2 relax chairs.
- 4P: double tent 2 × (265 × 265 × 230 cm), 2 double mattresses, 4 sleeping bags, 2 night lights, 2 mirrors, lock.
- Spectacular 4P: adds flooring, 2 power outlets, 4 relax chairs, 2 lockers.
- Easy Tent sleeping bags are one-size-fits-all.

# Montagoe (luxury glamping zone)
- Zone perks: dedicated catering, swimming pool, private bar, 24 h lobby team, free indoor showers; separate entrance between festival entrance and DreamVille entrance 2.
- All 2P; all include lighting, 220 V EU power, parking voucher (parking NOT included when bought via Global Journey).

# Mousai / Mansion (up to 10 people; ultra-luxury)
- Personal reception with welcome drink; Thursday BBQ buffet + cocktail; daily breakfast buffet 08:30–11:30.
- 5 bedrooms each with private bathroom; 80 m² private lounge; garden with jacuzzi (daily maintenance); Nespresso machine; fridge stocked with complimentary spirits/soft drinks/water/beer; housekeeping; butler; parking.
- Price from ~€60,000 + tickets; book via mansion@dreamville.be.
