# Shop structure

The official Add-On Sale runs on Paylogic. There are two identical shops, one per weekend (each shop banner cross-links to the other). Both contain seven tabs:

1. **Festival Food & Drinks** (dining experiences, with three sub-pages: Brasa, Tomorrowland Dining Experience, Belgian Classics Experience)
2. **DreamVille** (three sub-pages: DreamVille General, Magnificent Greens Area, Easy Tent Area, Montagoe Area — four cards in total)
3. **Lockers** (Festival Lockers and DreamVille Lockers sections)
4. **Parking** (Festival Parking and DreamVille Parking sections)
5. **Avicii** (The Avicii Tribute)
6. **Global Journey** (The Rhythm of Consciencia closing show)
7. **Summit** (Love Tomorrow Summit — redirects to a separate Love Tomorrow ticket shop)

City and airport shuttles are NOT in this Paylogic shop; they are sold via a separate official shop at event-tickets.be (linked from the Tomorrowland add-on pages). Included below for completeness.

General shop rules (stated in the shop):

- Add-ons never include festival or DreamVille entry; a valid ticket (and for DreamVille items, DreamVille access) is required.
- Orders cannot be changed after submission; groups who want to sit together must book in one order.
- Restaurants cannot accommodate special dietary requirements; menus may differ from photos.

# 3. Lockers

All lockers are operated by Lockerbox (their rental terms apply). Sizes (w × d × h approx.): Small 45 × 30 × 20 cm, Medium 45 × 30 × 32 cm, Large 50 × 30 × 40 cm, Extra Large 60 × 50 × 36 cm.

## Festival Lockers

Only accessible during festival opening hours; with a 3-day locker belongings may stay overnight. A cloakroom (not a locker) is provided for The Balcony, Rose/Orchid/Lotus Lounge, Skybox and Exclusive Table & Box guests. Limited stock; cheaper in advance than on site.

| Product | Price (EUR) | What's included / notes |
| --- | --- | --- |

| 3-Day Festival Locker | €25.00 (Small) | Whole weekend, overnight storage allowed; larger sizes cost more (exact figures unconfirmed, flag) |
