# Sale mechanics (2026)

- The 2026 Add-On Sale opened on Tuesday 16 June 2026 at 15:00 CEST (official FAQ and add-on hub). Historical pattern: 17 Jun 2025, 13 Jun 2024, 20 Jun 2023 — always mid-June, roughly five weeks before Weekend 1.
- Sold through the official Tomorrowland shop (Paylogic platform) at belgium.tomorrowland.com; buyers are admitted in random waves via a queue, exactly like the ticket sale. Joining the queue before 15:00 CEST does not guarantee early entry.
- A Tomorrowland account (my.tomorrowland.com) is required, but a ticket does NOT have to be attached to that account — anyone with an account can buy add-ons (third-party confirmation).
- Add-ons are bought per weekend — the shop separates Weekend 1 and Weekend 2 inventory (official "Book now for WEEKEND 1 / WEEKEND 2" links).
- Vouchers are limited, first come, first served (official FAQ). All sales are final: no refunds, no changes of weekend or timeslot after purchase (third party, consistent with official T&Cs).
- Add-on vouchers are not personalised — staff scan the barcode, so vouchers are transferable in practice (third party).
- Practical items (lockers, parking) remain on sale long after opening and are also sold on site, but on-site prices are higher (official pages state "tickets bought on-site are more expensive"). Restaurant tables and DreamVille breakfasts sell out within minutes.
- Add-on categories for 2026: Tomorrowland (any ticket holder), DreamVille (DreamVille access required), Global Journey (package holders only), Discover Europe (package holders only).

# 1. Dining / restaurant experiences (festival grounds, 2026)

Three pre-bookable sit-down experiences, all bookable for groups of 2–10 (no solo bookings); parties larger than 6 may be split across tables. Multiple timeslots on Friday, Saturday and Sunday of each weekend. No special dietary requirements accommodated; main course chosen at booking. Non-refundable.

| Experience | 2026 price pp | Location | Includes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Tomorrowland Dining Experience (Mainstage Restaurant, formerly "Mesa") | €89 | Hilltop overlooking the Mainstage | Welcome prosecco, appetiser, starter, main (poultry or veggie), dessert; water on table |
| Brasa BBQ Experience | €59 | Between Planaxis and Rise stages | Welcome cocktail, appetiser, grilled main (meat, fish or veggie), sweet bite; water |
| Brasa BBQ Deluxe | €105 | As above | Same format, upgraded all-meat menu (incl. Wagyu tasting per some coverage) |
| Belgian Classics Experience | €59 | Between Info Point and Freedom Stage | Welcome drink, bread and butter, appetiser, starter, main (beef stew, vol-au-vent or veggie), sweet bite |

- Prices above are from 2026-dated third-party coverage (Refined Trails); the official pages confirm the three experiences and formats but keep prices behind accordions. Treat as high-confidence 2026 figures.
- 2026 Mainstage Restaurant chefs (Michelin-starred): Roger van Damme, Nick Bril, Thijs Meliefste and Marcelo Ballardin; the menu follows the 2026 theme "Consciencia".
- Timeslots [2026, third party]: Mainstage Restaurant and Belgian Classics 13:15 / 14:50 / 16:10 / 17:30 / 18:50 / 20:10 / 21:30; Brasa (both tiers) 13:30 / 14:45 / 16:00 / 17:15 / 18:30 / 19:45 / 21:00 / 22:15.
- Demand ranking: Mainstage Restaurant sells out first (minutes), Brasa is competitive, Belgian Classics did not sell out quickly in 2025.
- Non-bookable premium food area: Papillon (near Freedom Stage) — walk-in street food by Chef Pam, wine bar with sommeliers, two mixologist-run cocktail bars, oysters, the Tomorrowland Waffle. A Papillon Cocktail Experience (about €39, one-hour mixology session in the Mesa Garden) has been reported by festivalviewer.com but is NOT confirmed on official 2026 pages — treat as unverified/possibly 2025.
- Note on names in the task brief: "B.EAT" and "The Tasting Journey" do not appear anywhere on official 2026 Tomorrowland Belgium pages — the 2026 line-up is the three experiences above. "Mesa Garden" appears only in unofficial coverage.

# 2. Lockers

Official 2026 page confirms 1-day and 3-day lockers, located on the festival grounds with access throughout the day; the price/measurement table sits in an accordion. Figures below match third-party 2026 guides and are unchanged from 2025:

| Size (cm) | 1 day | 3 days |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Small 45 × 30 × 20 | €7.50 | €20 |
| Medium 45 × 32 × 30 | €10 | €25 |
| Large 50 × 40 × 30 | €12.50 | €30 |

- Refined Trails' 2026 guide gives a loose "roughly €15–€40" range for festival lockers, which does not contradict the table when 3-day prices are considered, but the per-size figures above are the better-sourced set. Flag: per-size festival locker prices not directly confirmed on an official 2026 page.
- No charging lockers are advertised for 2026. Phone charging is a separate paid service ("Charge your phone" stations / power-bank hire with deposit) at both the festival and DreamVille; Camp2Camp guests can swap an empty battery for a full one at the Camp2Camp Lobby.

## DreamVille lockers — key-operated, 5-day only
Official 2026 page: only 5-day lockers are sold, accessible 24/7, located in the DreamVille area matching your accommodation. Prices below from 2026 third-party/fan sources (unchanged from 2025); each includes a €5 refundable key deposit:

| Size (cm) | 5 days |
| --- | --- |
| Small 45 × 30 × 20 | €25 |
| Medium 45 × 32 × 30 | €30 |
| Large 50 × 40 × 30 | €35 |
| Extra Large 60 × 50 × 36 | €45 |

- Lockable lodgings (Cabana, Kokono, Relax Room, Astera, Marivela, Ensuite) do not need one; DreamLodge has a small in-room locker; Spectacular (and reportedly Supreme) Easy Tent guests receive a complimentary locker voucher on arrival. [Carried over from 2025 sources; Supreme Easy Tent inclusion is third-party only.]
- Buy the locker for the zone matching your ticket type; lockers are also sold on site but with limited sizes.

# 3. Parking and mobility add-ons

- Confirmed on the official 2026 page. On-site purchase is more expensive (historically about €5 more per day). No overnight parking at festival car parks. Carpooling is encouraged (official page has a carpool section).

## DreamVille parking — official 2026 price
- Only 5-day parking vouchers are sold: €40. On-site purchase more expensive. [Official 2026.]
- 2025 rules likely still applying (flag as [2025]): single entry per voucher (leaving and returning requires a new one); complimentary parking voucher posted to Montagoe-tier guests unless booked via Global Journey; Friendship Garden / Easy Tent / Camp2Camp guests should buy Camp2Camp parking, Magnificent Greens guests the MG parking.

## Bicycle parking and drop-off (not add-ons — free)
- Free, staffed/supervised bicycle parking close to both the festival main entrance and DreamVille (official 2026). A free "Park & Bike" scheme lets drivers park at free remote car parks and cycle a safe route to the supervised bike parking.
- Kiss & Ride zone near the festival entrance for drop-off/pick-up only (no parking); taxis and pre-booked Uber/Bolt use the same zone. Free of charge, no voucher needed. [Official 2026.]

## Shuttles (sold in the add-on shop, per weekend)
- City shuttles (festival): operate Friday, Saturday and Sunday, round trip only, valid for one festival day; drop-off about 300 m from the Main Entrance. 2026 shop links exist per weekend; Brussels is promoted plus "other cities" across Belgium and the Netherlands (Antwerp, Ghent, Eindhoven etc.). [2025] price range was roughly €39–€51 round trip depending on city (e.g. Brussels Expo €40, Ghent €45, Liège €50, Rotterdam €49) — 2026 city-level prices not yet independently confirmed.
- Airport shuttles (DreamVille guests): inbound Thursday, return Monday only, from/to Brussels Airport (BRU), Brussels South Charleroi (CRL) and Eindhoven (EIN). [Official 2026 for routes/days.] [2025] one-way prices: BRU €26, CRL €33, EIN €33; 2026 coverage quotes a €25–€40 band. No refund or rebooking if a slot is missed.

# 4. DreamVille add-ons (2026)

## Breakfast (buffet, bookable per day or 3 days; timeslot chosen at booking)
Official 2026 pages confirm the three breakfasts and eligibility; prices from Refined Trails' 2026 comparison (flag: not price-confirmed on official pages):

| Breakfast | Eligibility | 1 day | 3 days |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Farmhouse | Magnificent Greens, Friendship Garden, Camp2Camp (open to all DreamVille) | €25 | €75 |
| Easy Tent | Easy Tent guests only | €29 | €87 |
| Montagoe | Montagoe guests only | €29 | €85 |

- Farmhouse timeslots run in 1.5-hour blocks: 08:00, 09:30, 11:00, 12:30 (format introduced in 2025, retained for 2026 per third party). Montagoe breakfast also runs on the Monday morning. On-the-day purchase costs roughly €34+ per day in pearls where available.
- Buffet contents (official): pastries and breads, cold cuts, hot items (eggs, sausages, bacon), pancakes (Farmhouse), seasonal fruit, yoghurt, cereals, smoothies and juices, coffee.

## Drinks
- Cold Beer Pick-Up: €30 for a pack of 24 cans of Belgian beer [official 2026]. Pick-up Thursday only, 11:00–17:00, at points behind both Magnificent Greens entrances. Incompatible with Global Journey Belgian Journey Thursday arrivals (they arrive too late). No ice is sold inside DreamVille.

## Camping gear rental (Magnificent Greens and Friendship Garden only; official 2026 page)
- Camp2Camp Tent Rental — reused, refurbished gear, ready on arrival (not pre-pitched when bought as an add-on rental package; pick-up at the Camping Store):
  - 1P Package: 1-person tent, single air mattress, sleeping bag, camping chair. [2025] voucher price €177 incl. €55 deposit.
  - 2P Package: 2-person equivalent. [2025] voucher price €271 incl. €80 deposit. (2026 add-on prices not yet confirmed; the separate Camp2Camp accommodation package — pre-pitched tent plus two Full Madness passes — prices the Camp2Camp element at €473 per 2P tent.)
- Party Tent (gazebo) rental: Tomorrowland-branded, pick up / return at a Dreamville Camping Store. 2026 third-party figure €175 including €125 refundable deposit (2025 fan-wiki figure was €195 incl. €150 deposit — conflict, flag both).
- Camping Chair rental: €45 including €25 refundable deposit [official 2026 page shows this figure].
- Gas bottles: NOT sold as an add-on and in fact prohibited — festival rules ban gas bottles/large gas canisters and open fire; cooking is only allowed at the provided public BBQ areas. Any older fact-sheet mention of gas-bottle add-ons should be removed.
- Trolley/cart hire: no 2026 (or 2025) evidence of a trolley or cart hire add-on. Visitors may bring their own trolleys to move gear; the Camp2Camp packages are the official "no gear" solution. Flag as not offered.
- Carrefour: there is a pop-up Carrefour supermarket inside the DreamVille Marketplace (higher prices than the Carrefour in nearby Rumst), but no Carrefour breakfast or grocery package appears as a 2026 add-on. Breakfasts are Tomorrowland's own buffets. Flag: "Carrefour packages" not part of the 2026 sale.

## Experiences (DreamVille)
- Secret Forest Yoga: €25 per session; mat and wireless headphones supplied; three timeslots — 09:30–10:15 Vinyasa Flow, 10:30–11:15 Power Flow, 11:30–12:15 Gentle Flow. [Official 2026 listing confirms the add-on; prices/slots from 2026 third party, unchanged from 2025.]
- Reading Session: The Legend of Consciencia — 2026 successor to the 2025 "Odes of Orbyz" reading session (official 2026 listing; price not yet confirmed, 2025 equivalent was €45 incl. book, snacks and drinks).

## Festival-side non-food add-ons (2026)
- The Avicii Tribute: returning for 2026 with new artefacts from Avicii's life; effectively free — €10 refundable deposit, returned on attendance; ~10-minute audiovisual show in a ~100-person container theatre; arrive 10–15 minutes early or risk refusal.
- Love Tomorrow Summit: listed as a 2026 festival add-on (talks/ideas programme); price not yet confirmed.
- Symphony of Unity: NOT listed among 2026 add-ons (was €25 in 2025 at Brussels venues). Flag as discontinued or unannounced for 2026.
