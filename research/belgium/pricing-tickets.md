# Ticket types (definitions)
- Regular: general festival access.
- Pleasure Pass: general access + Comfort Areas at Library and Freedom Stage (NOT Mainstage).
- Comfort Pass: general access + Comfort Areas at Mainstage, Library and Freedom Stage.
- Day Pass: one day (Fri, Sat or Sun); no DreamVille access.
- Full Madness: all three days; no DreamVille access on its own.
- All prices below are package totals (2P = total for two people) and include payment/service fees but exclude the Treasure Case fee.
