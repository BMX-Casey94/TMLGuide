# Festival basics
- Location: De Schorre recreation area, Boom, Belgium (between Antwerp and Brussels).
- Format: two festival weekends in July (Fri–Sun each), plus a third weekend in 2022 only.
- DreamVille (official campsite) opens the Thursday before each festival weekend.
- Around 400,000 attendees across both weekends (unique entries counted per day, so one Full Madness Pass = 3 "tickets").

# Ticket demand (historic facts)
- 2017: ~400,000 tickets sold; roughly 60% via Belgium-only sale; ~5 million people attempted to buy.
- 2019 (per Belgian press/TML spokesperson): ~200,000 sold in Belgian pre-sale, ~100,000 in worldwide pre-sale, ~70,000 in the worldwide sale.
- 2025: ~45,000 tickets were tied to Global Journey packages.
- Global Journey sale is generally the easiest route to a ticket (except top-demand items such as Montagoe lodgings); GJ still sells out within hours.
- Both weekends can be bought in one transaction, EXCEPT Global Journey (separate orders per weekend, and per package type). No dual-weekend ticket exists.

# Pearls (cashless payment)
- Bracelet-based payment; currency is "Pearls". 2025 rate: 1 Pearl = €1.82. [STALE — confirm 2026 rate]
- Top-up: cash, card, or online (before and during the festival).
- Online top-ups: unspent Pearls automatically refunded post-festival, minus a small fee.
- Cash/card only accepted at: DreamVille Carrefour supermarket, cigarette stands, a few special stands.
- Budget guide: roughly 15–30 Pearls/day covers non-alcoholic drinks and food.

# Entrances & site logistics
- Two festival entrances: DreamVille entrance (near Mainstage) and main entrance (near Freedom Stage).
- Non-DreamVille bracelets: main entrance only; leaving via main entrance = no re-entry the same day.
- Stage-to-stage walking distances are long — Mainstage to Freedom Stage can take ~20 minutes (more in crowds).
- Ferris wheel is free; access left of the Freedom Stage; closed in rain.
- Mainstage opening show daily around 17:00; identical end-show every night after the final set.
- One World Radio studio on site, next to the Cage Stage.

# Key policies
- Drugs: zero tolerance — bracelet removed and banned; sniffer dogs in use.
- No outside food/drink on the festival site (allowed in DreamVille only). No glass anywhere, including perfume bottles.
- Empty soft-plastic bottles allowed in; free water refill points at toilets.
- Selfie sticks allowed up to 1 m; large flags/totems and 360 cameras discouraged.
- Prescription medication requires a doctor's note.
- Touching decorations = bracelet confiscation.
- Comfort areas (Mainstage, Library, Freedom Stage) have separate drinks menus — no drinks in or out; card payment accepted inside; pool at some.

# Practical facts
- Lockers exist at both DreamVille (key-operated) and the festival (code-operated); on-site purchase possible, some sizes sell out.
- Free sunscreen at info points; ponchos sold ~6 Pearls at info stands/bars (no longer free).
- Free pocket timetable/map at info stands; official festival app supports artist notifications (10 min before sets).
- Taxi Brussels Airport → festival: roughly €100–150 depending on traffic/group size; night rates higher. Pre-booked taxis restricted to Kiss & Ride zones (far away).
- No public Wi-Fi at the festival or DreamVille.
- Secret restaurant inside the Mainstage: ~€15,000 for 12 people; no phones allowed.
- Food-experience vouchers usually go on sale mid-June and sell out fast.
- Global Journey flight rule: skipping the outbound flight without notifying info@globaljourney.com auto-cancels the return leg.
