# Facts
- Tomorrowland appoints official travel partners per country/region; the authoritative, current list is published only on tomorrowland.com.
- Travel-partner allocations open after the main sales in the sale sequence (step 8 of 9 in 2025, ahead of only the waiting list).
- Anti-scam rule for site content: tickets bought from any company not on the official list, or from third-party resale sites, carry a high scam risk and are the standard cause of refused bracelets.
- Historic reference: travel-partner allocations were released around early February in 2019.
