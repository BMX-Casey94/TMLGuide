# Access & basics
- DreamVille is open to guests of Magnificent Greens, Friendship Garden, Easy Tent, Camp2Camp and Montagoe.
- Two entrances: Entrance 1 (front) and Entrance 2 ("rainbow", rear, on Rumstestraat); both have ticket troubleshooting desks; parking near both.
- Walking distances: entrance-to-entrance 20–30 min; then another 10–15 min to the festival gate.
- Strictly no glass (including perfume bottles). Own food/drink allowed in DreamVille only (not on the festival site); informal limits (e.g. ~6 beer cans pp) rarely enforced.
- ATM usually available on site.

# The Gathering (Thursday opening party)
- DreamVille-guests only; runs all day, Belgian DJs by day, bigger names in the evening; own drinks may be brought from the campsite; bars on site.
- Frequent surprise guests in past years (e.g. Dimitri Vegas & Like Mike 2015, Afrojack & Tiësto 2016, Alok/DVLM 2023 W1, Nicky Romero/Afrojack/Amelie Lens 2024 W2).

# Facilities (recent editions; vary by year)
- First aid, hair salon, make-up, bars incl. Belgian Beer Café, food stalls, laundry, gym, volleyball and tennis courts, supermarket (Carrefour — card payment only, limited alcohol), butcher, chill-out zones, recycling club, power-bank swap (deposit; valid all weekend), jewellery/merch/camping/phone-repair stores, hammocks, free public BBQ areas (coal provided; disposable BBQs banned; no vegetarian-only BBQs), wellbeing classes (yoga, kung fu, fitness), inflatable obstacle courses.
- Tentenzo shop sells tents, chairs, sleeping bags, air mattresses, gazebos (~€40) at reasonable prices — some tents not fully waterproof.
- Many food/drink outlets stay open until ~02:00–03:00.

# Showers & toilets
- Free outdoor showers (unheated), open 24 h; some have warm water at night.
- Paid indoor showers (heated, private booths); free for Montagoe guests. 2025 details:

- Power sockets at indoor showers are for styling gear, not phone charging.
- Flushing toilets, free, cleaned regularly; free water refills at toilets.

# Security & theft
- Tent theft is common; use lockers (24 h access, key-based; buy in the add-on sale — sizes sell out on site), tent locks, and never leave valuables near the tent door (tents are sometimes slashed).
- Easy Tents come with a pre-installed lock.

# Camping tips (facts worth conveying)
- Filling pattern: Magnificent Greens usually fills from Entrance 2 towards Entrance 1 (orange zone next to Entrance 1 fills from that side).
- Wooden-plank paths suit luggage carts.
- Gazebos are taken down by staff in strong wind.
- Belgian rain is frequent (heavy rain in 2013, 2015, 2017, 2023); 2023 also exceeded 35 °C.
- Very little natural shade — practically no trees.
- Water is sold capless or in cans; keep your own caps or buy Carrefour bottles.
- Free shuttles Boom Station ↔ DreamVille (Thu–Sat inbound, Monday return).
- SNCB (belgiantrain.be) sells discounted Tomorrowland return train tickets to Boom; discount codes issued to the main buyer (2025: 1 code per DreamVille/Day ticket, 3 per Full Madness without accommodation; round trips only; GJ buyers excluded).
