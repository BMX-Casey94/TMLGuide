# Dietary information (based on previous editions — verify on site)
- Gluten-free options include: Belgian fries, fish & chips, frozen yoghurt, pokébowls (fish & veggie), most Thai dishes, Willy Nacho Classic, Secreto pork.
- Vegan options include: Love Tomorrow burger and kebab, vegan roti/bara (Surinamese), vegan red curry (Thai), vegetarian pokébowl, gelateria items (Pinky Ice Dream, Crispy Chocolate Dream, vegan waffles), Thai tofu wrap, roti wrap.
- Vegetarian options widespread: falafel pita/platters, houmous, cheese croquettes, veggie gyoza, margherita pizza, veggie arancini, spring rolls, bubble waffles, truffle ravioli, roasted cauliflower.
- Lactose-free is the most common flag (most grilled meats, pitas, wraps, Thai/Surinamese dishes).
- Staff can advise on allergens at each stall; recipes change yearly.

# Alcohol/drink policies (relevant facts)
- Water sold in cans or with caps removed; free refill points at toilets; empty soft-plastic bottles allowed in.
- Comfort areas have separate menus; no drinks in/out of comfort areas.
- Restaurant/food-experience vouchers go on sale ~mid-June and sell out very quickly.
