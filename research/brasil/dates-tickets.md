- **Sources:** brasil.tomorrowland.com (welcome, sales-info, festival-passes pages); press.tomorrowlandbrasil.com (May 2026); Veja/Gshow (Sep 2025); Festivalando (2026)

## Headline facts
- **There is NO Tomorrowland Brasil in 2026.** The edition planned for 2026 was postponed after the July 2025 fire that destroyed the Belgian Orbyz Mainstage (the Belgian stage ships to Brazil by boat; the rebuild timeline made 2026 impossible). Officially a postponement, not a cancellation.
- **Next edition: Tomorrowland Brasil 2027, 30 April – 2 May 2027, Parque Maeda, Itu, São Paulo.**
- Theme: "Consciência" — a multi-continent narrative starting at Tomorrowland Belgium (July 2026), continuing at Tomorrowland Thailand (December 2026) and culminating in Brazil.
- "Portal of Tomorrow by Bybit" event held 1 May 2026 at Parque Villa-Lobos, São Paulo, marked one year to go.
- Last edition: 2025 (5th edition, "LIFE" theme), 10–12 October 2025, Parque Maeda; 150+ artists across 6 stages.

## History (context)
- First editions 2015 and 2016 at Parque Maeda (announced by David Guetta at Tomorrowland Belgium 2014); hiatus (SFX Entertainment bankruptcy, then Covid); returned 2023 (sold out in hours), then 2024 and 2025 editions.
