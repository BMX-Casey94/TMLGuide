# Tomorrowland Brasil – Transport & Shuttles

- **Sources:** brasil.tomorrowland.com/pt/vendas-adicionais/shuttles; Festivalando (2024/2025 guides); DropDaily (2025); press.tomorrowlandbrasil.com
- **Gathered:** 2 July 2026
- **Staleness:** ⚠️ Fares and boarding points are from the 2024/2025 editions; 2027 shuttle details go on sale 24 September 2026 (Shuttle Pass sold in the WorldWide Sale).

## Getting there — the basics
- Parque Maeda, Itu has no central location and no mass public transport; the official shuttle is the recommended (and only authorised) transport dropping you ~400 m from the festival/DreamVille gates.
- Alternatives: private car or app-based rides (Uber etc.), which use designated drop-off zones; official reserved parking is sold as an add-on.
- Shuttle tickets are sold per city and per timeslot on the official site (platform ByInti); outbound and return times are chosen at purchase and 100% digital.

## Festival shuttle (day/Full Madness ticket holders)
- 2025 fares (return included): São Paulo, Barueri, Campinas, São Bernardo — R$199; Curitiba — R$509; Rio de Janeiro also offered (multi-city boarding was new for 2025).
- 2024 fare was R$180 (São Paulo only).
- Outbound slots roughly 10:00–20:00; returns roughly 21:00/22:00–01:30 depending on day.
- 2025 São Paulo boarding points: Novotel Center Norte (Av. Zaki Narchi 500, Vila Guilherme) and Hilton Morumbi (Av. das Nações Unidas 12901). 2024 used Holiday Inn Parque Anhembi and Hilton Morumbi.

## DreamVille shuttle (campers)
- 2025 fare: R$219 return (2024: R$200).
- Departs São Paulo from Holiday Inn Parque Anhembi or Guarulhos Airport (GRU).
- Outbound roughly 08:30–10:00 (2024 window listed as 08:30–22:00); returns 05:00–12:30 on the day after the festival.

## Sales pattern
- Shuttles historically go on sale alongside the worldwide ticket sale (2025: 8 April; 2027: 24 September 2026).
- Box-office/physical sales existed in 2025 at Teatro Renault, São Paulo; payment accepted included Visa, Mastercard, Elo, Amex, Pix and Google Pay.

## Global Journey
- Official travel packages (flight or bus + hotel or DreamVille + Full Madness Pass) sell earlier than the general sale (2027 cycle: 17 September 2026); a Global Journey simulator with pricing publishes nearer the sale.
