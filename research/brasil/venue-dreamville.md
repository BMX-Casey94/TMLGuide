# Tomorrowland Brasil – Venue & DreamVille Camping

- **Sources:** brasil.tomorrowland.com (DreamVille package pages); press.tomorrowlandbrasil.com; topingressos.com.br; Festivalando
- **Gathered:** 2 July 2026
- **Staleness:** ⚠️ DreamVille package prices below appear on the official site for the 2027 cycle but some may reflect the 2025 edition — verify at the Sep 2026 sale. Venue facts current.

## Venue
- Parque Maeda (also styled Fazenda/Arena Maeda), Itu, São Paulo state — a rural leisure farm park roughly 60 miles (100 km) from São Paulo city.
- Venue for every edition: 2015, 2016, 2023, 2024, 2025, and next in 2027.
- 2025 edition hosted 150+ artists on 6 stages, plus a drone show; no mass public transport serves the site directly.

## DreamVille (official campsite)
- On-site campsite adjacent to the festival; access bundled with a Full Madness Pass in DreamVille packages (per person, not per tent).
- DreamVille guests get exclusive events, notably "The Gathering" opening party the day before the festival starts.
- Accommodation families:
  - **Magnificent Greens** — bring your own tent (or order a tent package) and pick your own pitch.
  - **Easy Tent** — pre-pitched limited-edition Tomorrowland tent with air mattress and sleeping bag ready on arrival.
  - **Mística (glamping)** — spacious accommodation with proper beds and extra perks; tiers include Solaris, Astera, Grande, plus Cabana/Dream Lodge-style options in past editions.
