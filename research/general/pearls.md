## Top-up mechanics (Belgium)

- Fully cashless on the festival site and DreamVille; the bracelet (or e-ticket if bracelet not shipped) is the only payment method for food/drink.
- Exceptions: DreamVille Carrefour shop, cigarette stands and a few special stands take normal payment.
- Top up online via Tomorrowland Account (My Bracelet) with Visa, Mastercard, Bancontact or iDeal, or on-site at top-up booths with cash/card.
- Belgium: minimum top-up €20; multiples of €20; max €1,000 per transaction; account balance capped at €5,000.
- Winter: minimum top-up €25; multiples of €25; same €1,000/€5,000 caps.
- Bracelet must be activated first (12-character Bracelet ID + 6-digit security code) and linked to the Tomorrowland Account.

- Deadlines 2026: WE1 — 15 July 2026 23:59 CEST; WE2 — 22 July 2026 23:59 CEST.
- Bonus Pearls are spent only after the regular Pearls from that transaction are used.
- Bonus Pearls are non-refundable and cannot cover the refund fee.

## Refunds

- First top-up made **online** → automatic refund of unspent Pearls to the same card/account a few days after the festival.
- First top-up made **on-site** (or balance above the online amount) → manual refund via my.tomorrowland.com, tight deadline, up to ~6 weeks to process.
- Flat refund fee: **2 Pearls** per refund.
