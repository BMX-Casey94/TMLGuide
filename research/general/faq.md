# Tomorrowland General FAQ — Fact Sheet

- **Date gathered:** 2 July 2026
- **Sources:** tml.guide "Tomorrowland - General FAQ" (`/guides/information-faq`, last updated 3 June 2025), tml.guide Belgium guide (`/guides/summer-guide`), official faq.tomorrowland.com
- **Staleness flags:**
  - tml.guide FAQ was last updated June 2025 — Pearl rate quoted there (€1.82) is the 2025 figure; 2026 is €1.90.
  - Fees/deadlines (e.g. €350 Global Journey name-change fee, April deadlines) are as stated for the 2025 cycle — verify each season.
  - tml.guide FAQ does not explicitly cover age limits, lockers or re-entry; those need dedicated verification from official FAQ before publishing.

## Tickets & purchase

- Maximum **4 passes** per person per order (exception: large products such as Friendship Garden allow up to 10).
- Most Visa/Mastercard debit and credit cards work; ensure the bank permits international payments to Belgium. Official site keeps an accepted-cards list.
- Purchases are final — no weekend swaps; the only route is the exchange desk plus the waiting list.
- No public schedule for line-up/host announcements; best guide is prior years' timings.

## Personalisation

- Every ticket must be personalised (attendee details added) after purchase; instructions arrive by email.
- Missing the personalisation deadline risks the **entire order being cancelled**.
- Ticket names cannot be changed after personalisation; ID can be checked at any time on-site.
- Global Journey packages are personalised at purchase; name changes cost **€350 per name** until a deadline (usually ~April).

## Resale & refunds (official routes only)

- **Exchange desk** = the only official resale/refund channel; opens after ticket sales, refunds some or all tickets minus fees.
- **Waiting list**: opens via Tomorrowland Account once sold out; closes when full; grants access to possible waiting-list sales after the exchange desk closes (~April). No ticket guarantee.
- Third-party platforms (Viagogo, StubHub, Ticketswap, eBay) are explicitly unsafe:
  - A ticket can be sold multiple times; name on ticket proves nothing (names editable during personalisation window).
  - Sellers can still list the same ticket on the exchange desk after a private sale.
  - Even a physical bracelet can be reported lost by the main buyer — old bracelet invalidated, Pearls transferred to the replacement.
- Note: tml.guide describes the official platform as the "exchange desk"; there is no official partnership with Ticket Swap-style third parties. (Flag: the brief mentioned "official Ticket Swap" — per tml.guide this does not exist; only the exchange desk is official.)

## Payment in instalments

- tml.guide FAQ does not document an official instalment scheme (flag: verify on official FAQ — Tomorrowland has historically offered payment-plan options for some products such as Global Journey; confirm before publishing).

## Pearls (summary — see pearls.md)

- 2025 rate: €1.82/Pearl; 2026 rate: €1.90/Pearl. Bonus Pearls for pre-festival online top-ups; 2-Pearl refund fee; bonus Pearls non-refundable.

## Bracelets

- Bracelets ship in waves over several weeks; a tracking-link error simply means the parcel isn't prepared yet — normal.
- Bracelet not arrived/lost: collect a replacement at the **will call desk** (main entrance and DreamVille entrance) with e-ticket/order confirmation + matching ID.
- Global Journey travellers don't need the festival bracelet to board airport/hotel shuttles — hosts issue a separate shuttle bracelet.

## Getting there

- Global Journey shuttle hub is roughly **2.5 km** (about 1.5 miles) from the main entrance; the walk from Mainstage to the shuttles takes ~45 minutes.
- Many transport options (shuttles, trains, taxis) — covered in tml.guide's directions guide.

## Cash on site

- Cash is only useful for the DreamVille Carrefour, cigarette stands, a few special stands and person-to-person trading; cash and cards both work at top-up booths.
- Practical tip from tml.guide: check the Pearl amount charged at bars/food stalls — mischarges happen; query them immediately.
