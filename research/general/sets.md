# Notable Tomorrowland DJ Sets (Official Uploads) — Fact Sheet

- **Date gathered:** 2 July 2026
- **Sources:** YouTube (official Tomorrowland channel + official artist channels), tomorrowland.com/article/tomorrowland-belgium-2025-full-sets, djmag.com (129 sets from 2024), edm.com (40+ sets from 2023), tml.guide Great Library (`/library`, 442+ sets catalogued)
- **Staleness flags:**
  - Only video IDs marked **verified** were confirmed during this research (channel + ID checked). All entries marked *ID to verify* must be confirmed on YouTube before publishing — do NOT publish unverified IDs.
  - Tomorrowland periodically removes/re-uploads sets (rights issues); re-check availability before embedding.
  - Many pre-2016 "full sets" circulating on YouTube are unofficial fan uploads — Avicii, for example, has **no official full Tomorrowland set** on the official channel; only the aftermovies capture his performances.

## Key facts about official set distribution

- Full sets are officially published on the **Tomorrowland YouTube channel**, the **Tomorrowland App**, and (since 2025) **Apple Music**; some artists (e.g. Martin Garrix, Armin van Buuren) also publish on their own official channels.
- 2023: 40+ official full sets released; 2024: **129** official sets released; 2025: full sets confirmed on YouTube/App/Apple Music (incl. Martin Garrix, Armin van Buuren, ALOK, Anyma, Sara Landry, Amelie Lens, Axwell, Charlotte de Witte, Fisher, NERVO, Hardwell, John Summit, Eric Prydz, Miss Monique, Solomun, Swedish House Mafia).
- Official Tomorrowland uploads are tagged with metadata such as `EDITION: TL24BE`, `STAGE: MAINSTAGE`, `CATEGORY: VOD`.
- tml.guide's "Great Library" catalogues 442+ sets/radio shows but links out to official platforms rather than hosting audio; it includes an "Avicii: The Tribute" collection.

## Notable gaps

- **Avicii**: no official full-set upload exists; his 2011–2013 performances survive via the aftermovies and unofficial recordings. tml.guide hosts an "Avicii: The Tribute" collection. Use aftermovie + tribute framing, not fake "full set" links.
- Charlotte de Witte, Eric Prydz, Fisher and David Guetta had **no** official 2023 full-set uploads (confirmed absent from the 2023 batch) — their official releases are from 2024/2025.
