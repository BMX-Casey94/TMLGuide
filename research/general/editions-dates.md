## Tomorrowland Winter 2026 (already held)

- Winter runs annually in March; first edition March 2019.
- Bracelet doubles as festival entry, payment method **and ski-lift pass** (worn on left wrist).

## Tomorrowland Brasil

- **No 2026 edition.** The festival skips 2026 and returns **30 April – 2 May 2027** at Parque Maeda, Itu (São Paulo), theme "Consciência".

- 2027 pre-registration: 30 April 2026 – 23 September 2026 via Tomorrowland Account; sales from 17/21/24 September 2026.
- History: launched 2015; paused after 2016; returned 2023, then 2024 and 2025.
