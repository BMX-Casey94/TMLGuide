## The three collections (Solana blockchain)

1. **A Letter from the Universe**
2. **The Reflection of Love**
3. **The Symbol of Love and Unity**

- Also exists: **Golden Auric** — an official Tomorrowland NFT but **not** part of the Medallion set.
- NFTs are bought/sold with Solana (SOL) on Solana marketplaces (e.g. Magic Eden, Tensor); tml.guide links out to collection pages.

## Medallion of Memoria

- **Not an NFT itself** — it's the holder status earned by holding at least one NFT from **each of the three collections in one wallet**.
- Core perk: **life-long access to an NFT-holder pre-sale with a guarantee to buy 2 Tomorrowland Belgium tickets per year** (per Medallion). No guarantee of a specific ticket type.
- Max 2 tickets per pre-sale regardless of how many Medallions; multiple Medallions on separate Matrica accounts each get their own 2-ticket access; Medallions cannot be combined for more tickets.
- The Tomorrowland account holder must be personalised on one of the purchased tickets.

## Additional (non-guaranteed) perks seen historically

- Exclusive NFT ticket sales for Tomorrowland Winter (2023–2025) and Brasil.
- Exclusive Global Journey & Friendship Garden NFT sales.
- Friendship Deck access in front of the Mainstage; Secret Stage access (Belgium 2024).
- Night-Before behind-the-scenes tours (2022–2024), Mainstage Restaurant shifts, merch pre-sales, raffles, CORE tickets/airdrop, secret gigs at Winter.
- tml.guide stresses: these extras carry **no guarantee of repetition** — the only headline benefit is the ticket pre-sale.

## How access works

- Wallet (Phantom/Solana) must be linked to the **Matrica** platform, plus e-mail and Discord; Matrica is the **only** authorised verification platform — anything else is potentially a scam.
- Pre-sale access requires **pre-registration** per sale, plus a **snapshot** check that NFTs are still held.
- A Tomorrowland account must be **nominated afresh for every pre-sale**; once nominated it's locked for that sale.
- All announcements happen in the official Tomorrowland **Discord** (`#nft-announcements`) — no e-mail comms (blockchain is anonymous).
- Two separate registrations exist: Regular/Global Journey pre-sale vs Friendship Garden; a wallet/Medallion used for one cannot be reused for the other in the same cycle.

## Safety warnings

- Scam airdrop: a random NFT literally named "Medallion of Memoria" may appear in wallets — **do not click the link in its description**. Hide it, burn it, or send it to `tmltrash.sol`.
- Do not verify or buy through any platform other than Matrica and reputable Solana marketplaces.
- Do not transfer NFTs out of the wallet between registration and the sale — the snapshot will disqualify the entry.
- Do not change the main-buyer information on tickets bought via the pre-sale.
