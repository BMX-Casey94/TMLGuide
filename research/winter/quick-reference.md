## Visitor Experience Centre (Palais des Sports)
- Saturday & Tuesday: 08:00–21:00; all other days: 08:00–20:00.

## Mountain stage routes (summary — see local-transport.md for detail)
- Amare: Pic Blanc 1 from Rond Point des Pistes (first stop).
- Frozen Lotus: Marmottes 1 from Bergers.
- Reflection of Love: Signal from Rond Point des Pistes, or Grande Sure from Vieil Alpe.
- Friends by Coke Studio: Marmottes 1 from Bergers.
- L'Aventure by Corona: Pic Blanc 1, change to Poutran 2, intermediate station.
- Snowcat: mobile, roams the area.

## Buses & lifts
- Town buses free, no ticket; no Palais des Sports stop during festival week.
- TML shuttle Tue–Fri, every 10 min, 12:00–17:30, Rond Point des Pistes ↔ Rond Point de l'Europe.
- Free town lifts 08:15–18:00: Télécentre, Alpe Express, Huez Express.
- Regional bus: Transaltitude, Grenoble ↔ Alpe d'Huez.
