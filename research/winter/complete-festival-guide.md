# Tomorrowland Winter – Complete Festival Guide (Overview)

- **Sources:** tml.guide/guides/winter-guide (updated 15 Dec 2019); Tomorrowland Winter press office (Mar 2026); faq.tomorrowlandwinter.com
- **Gathered:** 2 July 2026
- **Staleness:** ⚠️ The tml.guide overview page is SEVERELY stale (written for the cancelled 2020 edition). Core structural facts still hold; specific dates/prices from it are obsolete. Current-edition facts below are from official 2026 sources.

## Current edition (2026 – most recent)
- Tomorrowland Winter 2026: 21–28 March 2026, Alpe d'Huez, France (6th edition).
- Circa 22,000+ attendees; 100–150+ artists across 8 stages (slopes and resort centre).
- 2026 debuted a brand-new Mainstage and the Orbyz stage (imported from Belgium).
- Alpe d'Huez sits on a south-facing plateau; resort at ~1,860 m, ski domain up to ~3,300 m; ~250 km of pistes.
- Travel proximity: ~1 hr from Grenoble, ~2 hrs from Lyon, under 5 hrs from Paris.

## Festival structure (stable across editions)
- Week-long format; all packages are 7-day or 4-day and always include a festival pass, a ski pass and lodging.
- Packages sold via a Simulator ahead of sale; payment is split (part at purchase, remainder over ~10 days).
- Add-on sale later covers comfort upgrades, ski lessons, gear rental, transfers, restaurant bookings.
- Optional flight or bus travel packages; otherwise self-travel.
- Lodging types: hotel, apartment or chalet.

## Historic facts (2019/2020 – stale, context only)
- 2020 edition (cancelled): planned 14–22 March 2020; 30,000 tickets; cheapest package was a 4-day, 4-person hotel at €686 pp.
- Package prices historically include tourist tax, all service/payment fees, and bracelet/gift-box shipping.
- Name changes: not possible after personalisation except urgent cases, with a €350 fee per change, until a set deadline.
- Shipping-address changes not possible after purchase (bracelets posted in advance).
- Official contact: info@tomorrowlandwinter.com (exhaust FAQ/community options first).

## Weather
- Highly variable: warm sunny ski days through to zero-visibility snowstorms in the same week; pack for wet, wind and snow.
