# Tomorrowland Winter – Accommodation

- **Sources:** tml.guide/guides/winter-accommodation (updated 22 Feb 2025)
- **Gathered:** 2 July 2026
- **Staleness:** ⚠️ Reflects the 2025 edition; categories/partners typically carry over but verify for the next edition.

## Package lodging (4- or 7-day packages)
- Choice of hotels, apartments and shared chalets, in four tiers:

| Tier | Level |
|---|---|
| Wood | Basic |
| Bronze | Standard (e.g. 3-star hotels) |
| Silver | Premium |
| Gold | Luxury |

- Hotel photos in the shop/simulator are examples only — check the hotel's own website for the room type.
- Apartment interiors vary widely; bed types can be anything from sofa beds and bunks to doubles.
- Shared Chalets: private room (sometimes en-suite) but shared living room and kitchen.
- Many shop apartments are named after the town sector they sit in; a sector map exists.

## Booking independently
- Alpe Locations Vacances rents apartments (whole weeks only); pairing with a Festival Pass can be slightly cheaper than a package, and you know the exact apartment in advance.
- Other agencies: Alpe d'Huez Houses, Maeva. Airbnb possible but hosts may cancel for higher bidders.
- Final cleaning, towels and bed linen are not always included — often payable extras; check listing descriptions.
- **Critical:** book inside Alpe d'Huez itself, NOT surrounding villages (Huez Village, Auris, Oz, Villard Reculas, Vaujany, Bourg d'Oisans) unless you have a car. Day lifts connect villages (covered by the ski pass) but there is no public transport at night and taxis are nearly impossible post-festival.

## Sunweb packages (surrounding villages)
- Sunweb (official travel partner) sells limited packages with lodging in Oz-en-Oisans or Auris via its own channels.
- **Must add the festival ticket AND the shuttle at time of booking** — they cannot be added later; without the ticket you have no night-stage access, and without the shuttle no way back at night.
- The Sunweb shuttle runs only on the main festival days (Wednesday–Friday).
