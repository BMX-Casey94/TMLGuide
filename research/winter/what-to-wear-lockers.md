# Tomorrowland Winter – What to Wear & Lockers

- **Sources:** tml.guide/guides/winter-lockers (updated 4 Dec 2024); locker prices from tml.guide/guides/winter-vouchers
- **Gathered:** 2 July 2026
- **Staleness:** ⚠️ 2025-edition prices; clothing advice is evergreen.

## Clothing strategy
- Layering is essential: mountain weather flips from warm sun to snowstorm within minutes.
- Indoor stages at the main festival area are heated — switch to lighter clothing for the evening rather than carrying ski wear all night.
- Leave parkas/ski trousers at your lodging for the night session; the walk to/from stages is chilly but short. Piling jackets on the dance floor is frowned upon.

## Temperatures
- Resort altitude 1,800 m; ski area peaks at 3,300 m.
- Night temperatures can hit −15 to −20 °C.
- Sunny daytime: roughly 0 to 5 °C; lower if snowing all week.

## Lockers
- Limited lockers at the Mainstage and Cage Stage, bookable in the add-on sale.
- Locker areas only accessible during evening main-festival-area opening hours.
