# Tomorrowland Winter – Transport Guide (Car, Train, Plane, Taxi, Luggage)

- **Sources:** tml.guide/guides/winter-transport
- **Gathered:** 2 July 2026
- **Staleness:** ⚠️ 2025-edition prices (Transaltitude, Ben's Bus, Altibus, taxis, parking); operators are stable but re-verify fares yearly.

## By car
- Famous 21-hairpin climb to Alpe d'Huez; easy drive, but snow chains are mandatory to carry (even if unused).
- Covered and open parking in town; some lodgings have their own. Biggest car parks are in the Bergers area.
- Covered parking, 7 days: €94. Many outdoor car parks are free — check for pay machines on site.
- Arriving late (4-day/day pass) risks full car parks.

## By train
- Nearest station: Grenoble (TGV from Paris), then Transaltitude bus (~90 min) to Alpe d'Huez.
- Pre-book the bus — boarding without a reservation is refused if full. Multiple stops in town; you can tell the driver your stop when boarding regardless of what you booked.
- Apartment-package guests usually check in at the Tomorrowland Desk at Palais des Sports — use the Bergers stop. The Palais des Sports and Eclose stops are not served during festival week.

## By plane
- Airports: Lyon and Grenoble (direct shuttles by Ben's Bus), Geneva (shuttle by Altibus).
- Ben's Bus Lyon shuttles run Saturdays only; Grenoble shuttles daily except Wednesday. Pre-booking required; shuttles wait briefly for delayed flights.
- Journey times: Lyon ~2.5 hrs, Grenoble ~2 hrs, Geneva ~4 hrs.

| Shuttle | Single | Return |
|---|---|---|
| Ben's Bus, Lyon | €48 | €87 |
| Ben's Bus, Grenoble | €39.50 | €69 |
| Altibus, Geneva | €65 | €75 |

- Lyon alternatives: TGV from Lyon Airport station to Grenoble, or Rhônexpress tram (every 15 min) to Lyon Part-Dieu + local train to Grenoble (hourly, cheaper, separate tickets), then Transaltitude. BlaBla Bus also runs Lyon Airport ↔ Grenoble roughly hourly.
- From Paris: train from the airport to Grenoble, then Transaltitude.

## Official travel packages
- 7- and 4-day bus or flight packages incl. all transfers. Buses from the Netherlands, Belgium, Switzerland, France; flights from Brussels, Amsterdam, Barcelona only. Departure times subject to minor change.
- Discover Paris package: 3-day Paris stay then coach to the Alps (see tickets-packages.md).

## Luggage rules
- Flight/bus packages: 1 suitcase (≤ 20 kg) + cabin bag + ski gear (flight packages: declare gear in the Tomorrowland Account before departure).
- Shuttles (incl. TML's) and Transaltitude buses include luggage; trains allow luggage — a name tag is mandatory in France.
- Avoid local trains at rush hour with lots of luggage.

## Departure warning
- Only one valley road serves both Alpe d'Huez and Les Deux Alpes: expect big traffic jams on departure day unless leaving very early; allow 1–2+ extra hours for flights/trains. Shuttle/bus timetables already pad for this, but add margin anyway.
