## Pattern takeaways
- Sales consistently run mid-to-late September, roughly 6 months before the festival.
- Order is always: B2B/hospitality → NFT pre-sale 7-day → general 7-day → NFT pre-sale 4-day → general 4-day → festival passes → (sometimes) day passes later.
- NFT (Medallion of Memoria) holders get pre-sale access.
