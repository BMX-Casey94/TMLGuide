# Tomorrowland Winter – Add-Ons & Vouchers

- **Sources:** tml.guide/guides/winter-vouchers (updated 4 Dec 2024)
- **Gathered:** 2 July 2026
- **Staleness:** ⚠️ All prices are from the 2025 add-on sale.

## Add-on sale basics
- Runs closer to the festival (after the main ticket sale).
- Covers: ski/snowboard lessons and experiences, equipment hire, ski insurance, activities (dog sledding, snowmobile, paragliding), lockers, airport/city shuttles, restaurant experiences, towels, Recharge Hub.
- Lesson and gear prices: see lessons-gear-rental.md. Locker prices: see what-to-wear-lockers.md.

| Snake Gliss | €22 |

- Timeslots are fixed at order time and cannot be changed — check for clashes.
- Weather cancellations are refunded by email notice.
- Highest demand (sell out in add-on sale): dogsled rides and paragliding. Paragliding not bookable as a pedestrian.
- Rail sledge: open all week to everyone, same price on-site at the ticket office next to Marmottes 1 (Bergers); multi-ride discounts on-site; VR experience +€4.
- Ice rink and swimming pools: pay directly on-site, no pre-booking.

## Recharge Hub
- Hut next to Marmottes 1 lift; Sunday–Friday, 09:00–13:30; 60-minute sessions.
- Relax Session €25 (incl. 1 drink) — slow down/reset. Recharge Session €25 (incl. 1 drink) — yoga flows and breathwork.

## Beauty Hub
- Free 20-minute Sephora make-up sessions, daily 12:00–15:00 and 19:00–21:00; second walk-in hub beside Amare during stage hours.
