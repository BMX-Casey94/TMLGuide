# Tomorrowland Winter – Tickets & Packages (incl. Discover Paris)

- **Sources:** tml.guide/guides/winter-tickets (updated 4 Dec 2024)
- **Gathered:** 2 July 2026
- **Staleness:** ⚠️ 2025-edition details; Discover Paris was "new for 2025" — confirm continuation for future editions.

## Ticket types
- 7-day Packages; 4-day Packages; 7-day Festival Passes; 4-day Festival Passes; Day Passes.
- Every ticket includes the festival pass AND a ski pass (mandatory — needed to reach mountain stages). Packages additionally include lodging in Alpe d'Huez.
- Day Passes exist for all days, but the Mainstage only opens Tuesday–Friday, so Sat/Sun/Mon day passes are cheaper.
- Recommendation: full week or at least 4 days to explore the whole area.
- Official simulator previews package + lodging combinations.

## Comfort Upgrade (+€375, 2025)
- Access to the Comfort Area on the Mainstage mezzanine and a dedicated area at Amare (subject to availability).
- Mainstage Comfort Area includes cloakroom, dedicated toilets and complimentary finger food.
- Also included: unlimited outdoor swimming pool access, unlimited ice-skating rink access, one free rail-sledge (alpine coaster) ride.

## Travel packages
- Flight and bus packages from various European departure cities; all include transfers, lodging, festival ticket and ski pass.
- Bus packages: cities in the Netherlands, Belgium, Switzerland, France. Flight packages: Brussels, Amsterdam, Barcelona only (2025).

## Discover Paris (new 2025)
- 3-day hosted Paris experience with other Winter attendees before the festival.
- Includes 3 nights in a 3-star hotel, breakfast, a surprise activity, and coach transfer Paris → Alpe d'Huez and back to Paris or Lyon Airport.
- Selected as an add-on at step one when ordering a 7-day or 4-day package.
- Prices (per package, not per person): single bedroom (1p) €1,050; double bedroom (2p) €1,400.
