# Tomorrowland Winter – Ski/Snowboard Lessons & Gear Rental

- **Sources:** tml.guide/guides/winter-lessons (updated 4 Dec 2024); pricing cross-referenced with tml.guide/guides/winter-vouchers
- **Gathered:** 2 July 2026
- **Staleness:** ⚠️ 2025-edition info and prices.

## Lessons
- Bookable via the official add-on sale, or directly on-site with local ski schools — the two largest are ESF and ESI.
- Durations: single half-day (morning or afternoon) up to the whole week.
- Group lessons or private (own group up to 5 people); levels: Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced.
- Timeslots start 09:30 or 10:30; to be grouped with friends, book everyone in one order.
- Club Med package guests get ski lessons included in the package price.

## Gear rental
- Pre-book via the add-on sale (4- or 7-day hire) or rent on-site; many sports shops in town.
- Hotels sometimes have discount agreements with nearby shops — ask at reception.
- Four gear levels: Beginner, Average, Advanced, Excellence. Each package: boots + skis/board (ski packages include poles).
- Clothing cannot be pre-booked via the add-on sale but can be rented on-site.
