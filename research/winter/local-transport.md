# Tomorrowland Winter – Local Transport & Reaching the Mountain Stages

- **Sources:** tml.guide/guides/winter-localtransport and winter-quickguide (both updated 4 Dec 2024)
- **Gathered:** 2 July 2026
- **Staleness:** ⚠️ 2025-edition stage line-up and lift assignments; stage names/lifts can change per edition (2026 added Orbyz).

## Getting around the resort
- Alpe d'Huez town is compact: max 15–20 minutes' walk between any two points; walking home after the festival is always possible.
- Free daytime buses for everyone, no ticket needed. Buses do NOT stop at Palais des Sports during festival week; Eclose stop also closed that week.
- Dedicated TML shuttle Tuesday–Friday, every 10 minutes, 12:00–17:30, between Rond Point des Pistes and Rond Point de l'Europe (links the Reflection of Love/Amare lifts with the Frozen Lotus lift).
- Free in-town lifts (open roughly 08:15–18:00, daytime only):
  - Télécentre ("lobster pods"): Place Paganon – Place Jean Moulin – Rond Point des Pistes (lower ↔ upper town).
  - Alpe Express: Place Paganon – Eclose – Bergers (old town Vieil Alpe ↔ Bergers).
  - Huez Express: Huez Village ↔ Alpe d'Huez.

## Reaching the mountain stages (2025 layout)
| Stage | Route |
|---|---|
| Amare | Pic Blanc 1 lift from Rond Point des Pistes, alight at first stop |
| Frozen Lotus | Marmottes 1 lift from Bergers |
| Reflection of Love | Signal lift from Rond Point des Pistes, or Grande Sure lift from Vieil Alpe |
| Friends by Coke Studio | Marmottes 1 from Bergers |
| L'Aventure by Corona | Pic Blanc 1 from Rond Point des Pistes, change to Poutran 2 at first stop, alight at intermediate station |
| Snowcat | Mobile stage — roams the area |

- All mountain stages are pedestrian-accessible.

## Stage-hopping realities
- Amare ↔ Frozen Lotus: different altitudes; 30 minutes minimum, budget ~1 hour with lift queues. The connecting Lièvre Blanc chairlift may close for crowd control and never carries pedestrians (they detour via town).
- To Reflection of Love: always descend to Alpe d'Huez first, then take the Signal lift up.
- Lifts close to pedestrians when a stage hits capacity (one-out-one-in); Comfort Pass holders usually get priority. Arrive early for headline sets; ideally don't switch mountain stages once in.
- Pedestrian tip: at the Lac Blanc aerial tramway (top of Pic Blanc 2), pedestrians may use the ski-school entrance left of the main queue to skip the line.

## Skiing notes
- Slopes for all levels; showpieces for advanced skiers: the tunnel slope and La Sarenne (16 km ≈ 10 miles, one of the longest black runs in the world).
- Lifts serving stages keep running after the last set until everyone is down — EXCEPT connections to surrounding villages, which have fixed last departures (shown on the pedestrian map).
- Expect 1-hour-plus lift queues after the final set, worst at Frozen Lotus.
