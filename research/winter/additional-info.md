# Tomorrowland Winter – Additional Information (Supermarkets, Signal, WiFi)

- **Sources:** tml.guide/guides/winter-additionalinfo (updated 4 Dec 2024)
- **Gathered:** 2 July 2026
- **Staleness:** ⚠️ 2025-edition info; low churn, likely still accurate.

## Supermarkets
- Five supermarkets in the resort: Utile, Intermarché, Carrefour, Spar, Sherpa.
- All are small-format but stock festival essentials and up their drink supplies for festival week (Carrefour historically best prepared).
- Additional small grocers and bakeries (boulangeries); French patisserie recommended.
- Check opening hours — some shops close early or shut over lunch.

## Mobile signal and WiFi
- Good mobile coverage across the whole ski area.
- Speeds slow at peak times, especially evenings when everyone is back in the resort; lodging WiFi is similarly limited — do not expect home-broadband performance.
- Non-EU visitors: buy a cheap French pre-paid SIM (e.g. Lebara, Lycamobile) online or at Tabac kiosks, then add a data bundle — mobile data often beats lodging WiFi.
