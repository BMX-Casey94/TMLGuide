# Tomorrowland Winter – Stages & Opening Times

- **Sources:** tml.guide/guides/winter-stages; press sources for 2026 stage changes
- **Gathered:** 2 July 2026
- **Staleness:** ⚠️ tml.guide content describes the 2025 layout. 2026 introduced a new Mainstage and the Orbyz stage (8 stages total in 2026).

## Main festival area (town centre, by Palais des Sports)
- Open from late afternoon/early evening throughout festival week; Mainstage itself opens only from Tuesday.
- Four stages in 2025: Mainstage, Cage, Moose Bar, Core — the first three are indoor and heated.
- Festival partners run stalls, some hosting DJ sets (e.g. Brasa).
- Opens 19:00; closes 00:00 (Sat–Mon) or 01:30 (Tue–Fri).

## Mountain stages (daytime)
- Multiple mountain stages, all reachable by lift — pedestrians included. Wear sturdy shoes; surfaces are icy.
- Open from noon, close 16:30 (Sat–Mon) or 17:30 (Tue–Fri).
- Smaller pop-up stages are listed in the Tomorrowland newspaper — not all appear in the official app.
- Lifts serving stages keep running post-set until everyone has left (except village connections with fixed last departures — check the pedestrian map).
- Long lift queues after the last set, notably ~1 hour+ at Frozen Lotus.
- Capacity control: one-out-one-in when full; pedestrian lift access suspended for full stages; Comfort Pass holders usually prioritised.

## Food & drink
- Same food quality/experience as Tomorrowland Belgium but fewer options; international dishes plus Belgian fries.
- Local speciality tip: Tartiflette (Alpine cheese dish).
- Pearls accepted at mountain stages and the main area; some mountain restaurants also take euros — compare, as Pearl pricing can be cheaper or dearer.
- 2025 rate: 1 Pearl = €2.77. Approximate: soft drinks ~2 Pearls, beer 2–3, wine 4–5, long drinks ~5, cocktails ~8, fries ~3, burgers/curries 7–8.

## After hours
- Afterparties in small town clubs run into the early morning, sometimes with big names — Igloo is the go-to.
- Private lodging afterparties are discouraged (noise).
