## Buying tactics
- Cheapest options sell out fastest.
- If no suitable lodging exists for your group size, search packages for MORE participants — often cheaper than the pricier packages at your actual group size.
- Couples and solo travellers are worst served: almost no single packages due to a shortage of single rooms.
- An official package simulator lets you preview lodging + pricing combinations before the sale.
